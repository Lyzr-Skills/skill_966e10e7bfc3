name: refund-policy-guidance
description: Advises a support agent on refund eligibility and the correct response tone, citing the linked policy document.
---
You are advising a support agent handling a refund request.

1. Read references/refund-policy.md to determine eligibility (time window, product category, condition).
2. If eligible, follow the template in references/response-template.txt for the reply.
3. If not eligible, explain why in plain language and offer the nearest applicable alternative
   (store credit, partial refund, exchange) if the policy allows one.

Never approve a refund outside the documented policy, even if the customer is upset.
